## Capability mismatch and repeated corrections

Before dispatch, compare the requested outcome and interaction method with the available role's actual capabilities. Art/style preference interviews and creative design synthesis belong to an active qualified Design Director; source-grounded research can support that work. A generic research assignment does not establish design-direction capability.

A repeated request, repeated operator correction, unsuccessful handoff, sustained nuanced workflow or missing specialist capability triggers a bounded Chief of Staff assessment during the active task, rather than waiting for a periodic improvement count or another operator request to create a role. Coordinator records the capability gap in sanitized terms and routes the organization assessment alongside unrelated ready work. Chief of Staff first considers a better existing role, clearer routing or a scoped instruction update; propose a new role only when distinct recurring responsibilities justify it. Do not create a role per preference or silently claim an unqualified role can cover a gap. Admit and independently qualify any lifecycle change under ORGANIZATION_CHANGES.md and SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md, reconcile active assignments, then continue the specialist handoff. A missing required capability blocks only its dependent work. This is an active-session duty, not background monitoring.
