Chief of Staff governs improvement within operator authority; no routine human approval is needed for qualified organization changes. Coordinator considers proposals at active-session startup and after five accepted substantive tasks. Triggers are proposals/admissions, not permission or a permanent worker service.

Before results, register hypothesis, target, practical claim, baseline/candidate/evaluator identities, representative workload, metrics/denominators/window, stopping rule, guardrails, cumulative budget and known-good rollback. Pin approved instructions/policies/evaluator and independently specified acceptance criteria. Keep failed/retried/missing observations and raw sanitized artifacts. Never improve the scoreboard by changing criteria/history/denominators for the same candidate.

For deterministic fixes retain baseline failure, candidate pass and relevant regression suite. For stochastic benefit use at least 20 representative paired cases, randomized ordering, fixed stopping, a 95% paired interval with positive benefit bound and at least 5% practical relative efficiency improvement. Require no observed authority/evidence/safety failure, no new required-test regression and no degraded product correctness. Missing telemetry, insufficient samples, changed workload or exhausted budget is inconclusive, not promotion or a routine waiver request.

Fresh Reviewer examines pinned artifacts; independent Chief of Staff approves proven benefit; Coordinator reconciles scope/intent and mandatory PR integration. Evaluator/promotion changes are separate candidates governed by previous approved evaluator, adversarial fixtures and dual-version replay; new semantics never validate their own adoption. Reject failures, retain/refine inconclusive candidates in new recorded experiments under shared budgets. Verify exact head after merge. On regression stop new candidate use, reconcile active runs and apply known-good corrective/revert PR. Qualification of a harmless installation correction proves the lifecycle only; synthetic tests prove no production productivity claim.

## Event-driven lessons and capability review

Operator corrections and the capability-gap triggers in [routing](ROUTING.md#capability-mismatch-and-repeated-corrections) require assessment during the current authorized task; the periodic five-task proposal cadence is not a reason to defer them. Chief of Staff owns the assessment, Coordinator ensures admission and follow-through, and the applicable specialist owns the generic operating repair. Use existing capabilities before adding roles; preserve independent lifecycle review and reconcile affected active work. Record an explicit qualified repair, evidence-limited proposal or concrete blocker instead of only promising future compliance.

Apply [record ownership](RECORD_OWNERSHIP.md#learn-the-method-retain-decisions-with-their-project) before storing a lesson: improve the reusable method without importing project answers, identifying evidence or installation state. For a deterministic instruction repair, apply the existing baseline-failure, candidate-pass and regression requirements above; those observations establish only the bounded correction, not measured stochastic productivity or universal compliance. Any broader stochastic benefit claim still requires the unchanged paired-trial evidence above. Preserve prior evaluator and approval gates.

## Root-cause diagnosis and repair

Every observed unexpected failure, defect, regression, operator correction, scope/authority error, unsuccessful handoff or recurring incident receives containment, retained evidence and bounded causal diagnosis during the active task, including first occurrences. Perform steps 1–3 without delaying required ownership assignment below. Expected negative-test refusals and merely unfinished planned work are not new failures by themselves. Record every observation in the existing owning task or incident family; successful recovery does not erase it. Project implementation and raw evidence remain with their owner.

Coordinator ensures containment within existing authority and an accountable diagnosis owner. As soon as available facts show any exclusion—authority/evidence/safety impact, failed control, recurrence, uncertain cause, capability mismatch, failed correction, operator correction or proposed organization repair—or local eligibility is unknown, assign Chief of Staff immediately, **before** further verification retries or causal probes. Authorized containment and bounded source/verification checks may continue under that ownership; their success does not retroactively remove escalation. Chief of Staff retains organization-level assessment even when the symptom appears in a project. Do not wait for complete diagnosis to assign ownership.

The owning task may resolve an issue under ordinary task review only when it is isolated, explained, reversible, corrected within existing authority and locally verified, with every exclusion evidenced absent. Record the cause, correction and verification. Unknown eligibility is not harmlessness. Local resolution retains ordinary review/delivery duties; it neither qualifies an organization change nor establishes general prevention.

Here, operator correction means correction of agent conduct, interpretation, routing, workflow or failed work. A harmless direct nonsemantic documentation-edit request is not itself such a correction or an operating-method repair. A known content defect, including a harmless typo, still requires concise task-lead diagnosis when eligible: retain expected/observed evidence, cause, correction and verification. Absence of a conduct correction does not erase the defect. All organization content edits still require normal qualification, independent Reviewer/Chief of Staff approval and PR/main delivery. Never relabel an actual agent-caused failure or failed control as an edit request; every other exclusion still applies.

Use the existing task and correction queue, not another incident system. Simple problems may need only a short evidenced causal chain; consequential or recurring problems need proportionate depth. Do not invent a root to close work. Steps 4–6 govern escalated cases and every proposed organization repair or organization-level no-change finding; their independent qualification and recurrence duties remain mandatory. Unresolved diagnosis stays owned and nonterminal; unrelated authorized work continues.

1. **Establish the problem.** Record expected versus observed behavior, impact, instruction/source/candidate/environment identities, relevant sequence, original authority and evidence limitations. Distinguish facts, inferences and missing observations. Preserve the baseline failure and all failed attempts; a successful replay does not erase the incident.
2. **Trace the causal chain.** Separate the immediate trigger, proximate decision/action, contributing conditions, systemic causes and the control that should have prevented or detected escape. Use successive whys, a causal tree or another appropriate method. “The agent made a mistake” is an observation, not a sufficient systemic explanation. Check instruction conflicts, admission scope, incentives/persistence, context availability, review framing, capability/routing and tool/evaluator behavior where relevant. Do not assume each category is defective.
3. **Diagnose rather than narrate.** Name competing explanations and discriminating checks; test the counterfactual that removing the proposed cause would have prevented or detected the failure. Stop at evidenced, actionable causes under a clear owner; going deeper without evidence creates hypotheses, not certainty. If observations cannot distinguish causes within budget, preserve an owned nonterminal investigation with exact missing evidence, blocker and next safe check. Do not invent a root to close the task.
4. **Repair causes and escape controls.** Map each supported cause/control failure to a proportionate durable corrective action, owner, authority, acceptance evidence and rollback. Address the general organization method under standing authority, not only the local symptom or a promise to be careful. Keep project-specific preferences, code and raw source with their owner; generalize only evidenced methods. If no organization defect/change is justified, record the evidence and applicable detection/recovery response for independent review. Do not manufacture a universal rule or mutate unrelated systems merely to claim an organization fix. A needed action outside authority remains a named blocker; this loop supplies no extra grant.
5. **Qualify independently.** Preregister the causal hypothesis, previous approved evaluator, baseline/candidate, reproduction, adversarial and ordinary-work counterexamples, stopping rule and cumulative resources before results. Apply the existing deterministic or stochastic requirements unchanged. A policy edit alone does not establish behavioral correction; use fresh execution where behavior is the claim and retain all outcomes/limits. Test that the new instruction stops the failure without needlessly blocking ordinary autonomy. Reviewer independently checks diagnosis, responsibility/authority fit and repair evidence; distinct Chief of Staff decides the exact candidate under prior promotion rules. Failed/inconclusive repairs remain unpromoted.
6. **Deliver and verify recurrence prevention.** Complete normal PR/owning-remote main verification before calling the repair delivered. Close the corrective action only with a supported diagnosis or independently justified no-change finding, qualified remedy, independent approval, verified delivery where changes exist, and an explicit follow-up owner/trigger. Containment, reminders, new wording, local tests or a merged patch alone do not establish prevention. During subsequent eligible active work, record whether the countermeasure was available, loaded, applied and effective, observed recurrence and eligible exposure counts; unknown denominators stay unknown. Use existing startup/task/handoff checkpoints, not an unauthorized background service or schedule.

A recurrence reopens the prior diagnosis and countermeasure: retain its incident-family link, inspect whether the fix was absent, unapplied, inadequate or based on a wrong cause, and test a revised candidate under the prior rules. Do not relabel incidents, reset counters/budgets or replace failed evidence to preserve a success claim. “Prevent the same problem from recurring” is the required objective and verification duty, not a guarantee that instructions universally enforce behavior. Report bounded evidence, remaining exposure/causal uncertainty and any concrete blocker honestly. The parent cannot claim full problem resolution while a mandatory cause/remedy remains unresolved; unrelated authorized work continues.
