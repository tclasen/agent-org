- Target System: organization
- Change Classification: organization

- Owning Role: Chief of Staff
- Related Requirements: REQ-000001, REQ-000008, REQ-000011, REQ-000012, REQ-000013, REQ-000015, REQ-000016, REQ-000034
- Related Tasks: TASK-000002
- Approval Requirements: APP-000001; future revisions follow policies/APPROVALS.md

Operator grants define the outer authority. APP-000001 authorizes approved installation, native delegation, scoped Git/PR delivery and recursive evidence-qualified organization evolution; it does not pre-accept test outcomes. Preserved intake pending language is historical. Current explicit operator constraints and sandbox/tool restrictions prevail. Role instructions, plans, triggers, tools and project overrides cannot enlarge a grant.

Chief of Staff independently approves evidence-qualified organization changes autonomously; Coordinator admits tasks and reconciles intent and outcomes. Implementer, reviewer and approver must be independent executions without implementation authorship. Project authority is the operator until an explicit enrolled owner/delegate and bounded grant are recorded. Cross-boundary work needs both authorities for their respective obligations. Existing authorization persists: do not ask the operator again for ordinary organization maintenance within the grant.

Missing, expired, revoked, ambiguous or stale authority blocks its dependent action. Continue unrelated authorized work and report the exact missing grant. Production, deployment, purchases, external messaging, new providers and real legacy enrollment are outside the installation grant. Final installation acceptance belongs to the operator after the gap interview. No self-improvement may change these limits.
