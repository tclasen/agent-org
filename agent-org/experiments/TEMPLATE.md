# Improvement experiment template

- ID: EXP-<allocated>
- Target System: organization
- Change Classification: organization
- Authorized Scope: exact organization candidate paths; product changes need linked project grant
- Affected Paths or Resources: candidate paths
- Owning Role: Chief of Staff
- Related Requirements: accepted IDs
- Related Tasks: task IDs
- Approval Requirements: prior-evaluator independent approval
- Status: proposed

## Preregistration

Record hypothesis, scope, baseline and candidate hashes, prior evaluator/criteria/workload hashes, allocation/randomization, complete workload, target metric, practical threshold, guardrails, missing-data policy, stopping rule, cumulative budget and rollback before outcomes. Deterministic repair requires independently specified baseline failure and candidate pass. Stochastic efficiency claims require at least 20 paired cases, fixed 95% paired interval and 5% practical benefit; unknown target data is inconclusive.

## Results

Retain every outcome, failure/retry and resource observation; unknown stays unknown. Do not change denominator/history/criteria to pass.

## Independent review and disposition

Reviewer and approving Chief of Staff distinct from implementer; approval binds exact candidate under prior rules. promote/reject/inconclusive with evidence. PR required; changed head invalidates qualification.

## Rollback

Known-good identity, trigger, reversible scope, verification and active-task reconciliation. No product side effects authorized.
