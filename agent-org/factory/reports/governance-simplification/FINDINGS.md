A direct harmless nonsemantic typo-edit request differs from correction of agent conduct/failed work; normal qualification, independent Reviewer/CoS approval and PR/main gates still apply. Never relabel an actual agent-caused failure or escaped control as a mere edit. Unknown eligibility escalates.

They are not silently converted into new organization rules. Independent final review/CoS assess the no-additional-policy disposition under the pinned prior evaluator. External publication/merge approval restrictions remain binding; agent-org cannot repair them by bypassing the host.
