- Status: active
- Inputs: Every observed unexpected problem/correction (including first occurrence), or proposed organization hypothesis; user authority, approved baseline/prior evaluator and shared budget
- Outputs: Evidenced local resolution where eligible; otherwise owned organization assessment and independent bound approval/reject/inconclusive, with verified promotion/rollback if qualified
- Refusal Cases: Candidate evaluates itself, moved denominator/history, self-granted authority, missing target telemetry, guardrail regression or exhausted budget
- Evidence: Registered plan, baseline/candidate/evaluator/workload hashes, all outcomes, reviewer/approver independence and PR/rollback provenance
- Exit Criterion: Only evidence-qualified exact candidate promoted; rejected/inconclusive candidates stay unpromoted; rollback restores verified known-good behavior

## Procedure

1. Apply the canonical [first-occurrence diagnosis and resolution rules](../policies/SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md#root-cause-diagnosis-and-repair) during the active task. Coordinator ensures containment and diagnosis ownership. Retain every observation. Eligible local issues remain in the existing task under ordinary review; exclusions route to Chief of Staff. Do not turn a local resolution into organization qualification.
2. For organization assessment/repair, Chief of Staff selects the bounded hypothesis and follows the canonical [preregistration, prior-evaluator, qualification and rollback requirements](../policies/SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md), including its independent Reviewer and distinct Chief of Staff decisions. Those requirements remain controlling; this workflow supplies no alternative thresholds or waiver. Preserve approved instructions/evaluator and all outcomes. No project write comes from this workflow; a needed project change requires its separate authority/task.
3. Coordinator reconciles authority/intent and integrates a qualified exact candidate through [mandatory PR delivery](WF-000003.md). Bind candidate/evidence identities, recheck affected changes and verify owning-remote main under [acceptance](../policies/ACCEPTANCE.md#remote-main-completion-gate). Apply canonical diagnosis/recurrence follow-up and rollback; neither delivery nor a local recovery proves nonrecurrence.
