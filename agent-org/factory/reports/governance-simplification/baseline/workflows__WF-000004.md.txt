## Procedure

1. On every observed unexpected problem, Coordinator contains within authority and Chief of Staff begins bounded full root-cause diagnosis during the active task under SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md#root-cause-diagnosis-and-repair; do not wait for repetition or the periodic cadence. Preserve expected/observed evidence, causal chain, systemic/control-escape findings, alternatives and uncertainty. Link recurring incidents to the prior countermeasure and reopen it. Chief of Staff selects a bounded corrective organization hypothesis under operator authority. Before outcomes register claim, scope, baseline/candidate/prior evaluator, metrics/denominators/window, representative workload, guardrails, stopping rule, cumulative budget and known-good rollback.
2. Preserve approved baseline/instructions/policies/evaluator and independently specified acceptance. Implement candidate in assigned organization scope. No project write comes from this workflow; a needed project change becomes a separately authorized child.
3. Compare deterministically with retained baseline failure/candidate pass/relevant regression, or use preregistered stochastic paired workload of at least 20 cases, randomized ordering, fixed stopping, 95% paired positive benefit interval and at least 5% practical relative efficiency improvement. Keep failed/retried/missing observations.
4. Reject any observed authority/evidence/safety failure, required-test regression or product correctness degradation. Missing measurements, insufficient samples, changed workload or budget limit yields inconclusive and no promotion. Preserve outcome; a new experiment cannot reset parent budget or rewrite history.
5. Fresh Reviewer evaluates exact candidate under previous approved gate. Independent Chief of Staff records qualified autonomous approval, or reject/inconclusive; no routine human waiver substitutes for proof. An implementer execution cannot approve itself. Evaluator/promotion changes are separate candidates with prior evaluator, fixed adversarial fixtures and dual-version replay.
6. Coordinator reconciles authority/intent and follows mandatory PR workflow WF-000003. Bind head/tree/evidence hashes; changed head invalidates approval. Verify merge. On regression stop new use, reconcile active runs and submit known-good corrective/revert PR; retain failed evidence. Installation correction proves lifecycle only. Problem resolution additionally needs supported causal/repair evidence or an independently justified no-change finding, owned unresolved gaps, and recurrence follow-up at subsequent eligible active-work checkpoints. A policy edit, containment or delivery alone cannot prove nonrecurrence. Keep observed exposures/recurrences and unknown denominators; no background watcher or extra authority is created.
