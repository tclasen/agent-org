## Prioritized candidates, contrary evidence and bounded next tests

### C1 — Narrow first-failure routing and no-change closure

**Target:** SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md lines 28–37; AGENTS.md first-problem paragraph; WF-000004 procedure steps 1 and 6. Keep immediate containment, evidence retention and authority checks for every actual unexpected failure. Replace universal CoS routing and independent no-change closure for an isolated, fully explained tool/input mistake with proportionate resolution in the existing task, subject to its existing review. Escalate recurrence, material harm, authority/evidence failure, an unsupported cause, failed existing control or a proposed organization repair through the current CoS path. No new queue, role, record family or service is proposed.

**Why:** A07 currently requires organization-owned diagnosis even on the first parser error; SELF_IMPROVEMENT line 37 also conditions closure on independent no-change justification where no organization edit is warranted. Reading a wrong known path could therefore create more follow-through than its corrected local lookup merits. This is a textual mechanism, not an observed execution cost. The admission's recorded wrong-path lookup is an observation of a contained local mistake, not proof it was mishandled.

**Contrary evidence:** Lines 30 and 34 explicitly permit a short causal chain, bounded depth and stopping at evidenced actionable causes; line 35 forbids manufacturing a universal repair. Existing-task grouping and continued unrelated work already limit burden. The operator explicitly wanted first occurrences addressed; any narrowing must preserve first-occurrence evidence and repair actual systemic defects rather than treating recurrence as the only trigger.

**Risk/test:** A seemingly isolated authority error could escape. Before promotion, replay ordinary missing-path/parser/transient-tool cases alongside first-occurrence authority escape, repeated mismatch and uncertain-root cases in fresh executions. Score correct escalation, retained evidence, appropriate scope and unnecessary blocking separately. Retain baseline failures or absence of failures; do not claim efficiency without observed measurements. Qualify the change under the unchanged prior evaluator.

**Why:** The same thresholds/closure duties can drift across a policy and workflow; RESOURCE_BUDGETS already contains an explicit supersession note about older repeated worker limits. **Risk/test:** Use ordinary deterministic correction, stochastic-benefit proposal, failed guardrail and evaluator-change cases. No new navigation document is required.
