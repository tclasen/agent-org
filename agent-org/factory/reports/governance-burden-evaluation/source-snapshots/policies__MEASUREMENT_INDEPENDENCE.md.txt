Separate implementation, fresh review and approval executions. Pin baseline/candidate, workload, evaluator/definitions/classification versions and snapshot hash before comparison. Candidate cannot choose a favorable replacement evaluator, erase failed attempts or move denominator/window/stopping rule after observing results. Evaluator/promotion changes require previous approved rules, fixed adversarial fixtures, independent review and dual-version replay in a separate candidate.

Record source event IDs, occurrence/ingestion time, canonical commit/build/artifact/deployment/failure/recovery links, input/output/instruction/policy identities and transformations. DORA facts require explicit enrolled service/environment definitions and causality; never infer authority or failures from time proximity/narrative. Corrections append with causal references; accepted snapshots remain reconstructable. Unknowns/missing follow-up/denominators are explicit. Live registry begins empty; synthetic 100% traffic and 30-minute stabilization belong only to labeled fixtures.

Separate organization behavior, per-project quality/outcomes and measurement-health scorecards. Count observable instrumented operations and report direct tools/connectors/subagent internals as bypass gaps unless explicitly imported. Requested configuration, self-report and unavailable metadata are not trusted actual-model/cost evidence. No accepted claim of universal interception, hostile-worker isolation, immutable ledger or universal correctness: shared filesystem/native identity limit assurance. Independent verification and hashes detect covered changes, with trusted-anchor absence disclosed.
