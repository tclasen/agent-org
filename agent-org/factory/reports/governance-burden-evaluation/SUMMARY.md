## Findings

The selected 26-file corpus contains 13,237 whitespace-delimited words, including 1,190 header-metadata words (9.0%). This is a selected corpus, not the entire organization. Source-defined static startup is 1,635 words across five files for Researcher and 2,623 across six for Coordinator, excluding the 75-word root bridge, host instructions, task materials and triggered sources. The core direct-action table exposes 26 conditional destinations; compliant workers must not load them all. The structural route census covers 41 distinct organization documents in total. All preregistered 26 hashes match; additional route/read inputs were observed after execution and are labeled separately.

The clearest bounded subtraction targets are 490 historical words co-located with a 297-word reusable handoff contract, and 534 excess words in four identical paragraphs repeated across four sampled roles. The latter is a corpus total, not a predicted per-run saving. Replacement links and canonical placement affect actual net savings. Complete defaults in metadata also repeat, but validators, standalone scope and traceability depend on them; changing defaults is a higher-risk proposal with unestablished benefit.

The process analyst traced all twenty cases to exact excerpts, lines and hashes (ten ordinary and ten adversarial). Current policy already permits advice-only answers without mutation, direct instruction corrections without a recursive planning gate, routine implementation choices under a valid build grant, and continued unrelated work when a dependency is blocked. Simplification should preserve these existing autonomy provisions, plus project/effect authority, independent review, honest evidence and remote-delivery checks. Broad first-failure CoS routing and duplicated improvement procedures are plausible burden mechanisms, not observed causal findings.

Both behavioral conditions returned twenty responses in their frozen randomized order without reported scenario actions. The independent fixed-rubric scoring gives each condition **18/20 complete next actions**, **20/20 scope/authority**, **20/20 avoidance of unnecessary operator permission**, and **20/20 evidence honesty**, with no scored critical violations. Both omitted required Chief of Staff routing in the first-failure case. Baseline also omitted part of the responsibility-delta/supported-alternative response; candidate instead omitted Chief of Staff ownership in the capability-assessment case. Those are retained incomplete decisions, not erased successes or proof of safe execution. Ambiguous permission booleans were interpreted with their explanations and remain flagged. There is no observed decision-score advantage for the navigation aid in these two sessions. These omissions may reflect concise response reporting rather than actual task behavior; the pilot does not establish that the models would fail to route live work or why an omission occurred. Independent criterion scoring and any exceptions are in SCORES.json and REVIEW.md; no result is inferred from the analyst case traces. Requests used the advertised gpt-6-luna/medium routine-trial configuration without a reported invocation error. The tool did not supply trusted actual model, reasoning, capacity, token or cost metadata. These are two clustered sessions, not forty independent runs; the existing policy requires at least twenty representative paired cases for stochastic-benefit qualification, plus its unchanged interval, benefit, guardrail and regression criteria. This pilot supplies no justified independent-pair statistical analysis or broad improvement claim.

The optional navigation treatment added 159 words and replaced no mandatory instruction. Post-hoc counts of worker-reported paths sum to 6,182 governance-source words for baseline and 6,641 for candidate, with 791 and 950 words of task material respectively. These are self-reported read footprints, not measured prompt occupancy, token use, elapsed time or total work. They demonstrate no reading-volume reduction in these observations. Order, retrieval choice and clustered sampling limit causal interpretation. No navigation layer is recommended for adoption from this pilot.
