# Improvement candidate

Use `learning.py scaffold` for the checked metadata fields. Candidate, Parent, Content Hash, Incident Family, Partition, Exposures and Status describe searchable evidence; they are not formal task IDs or promotion permission.

Record the source incident and revision, task/run evidence, causal hypothesis, affected task classes, owning role, authorized mutation surface, counterexamples, independently specified qualification cases, attempted variants, resource observations and unknowns, exposure history, disposition and rollback. Link the admitted experiment and prior evaluator. Keep failed/rejected/inconclusive ancestors. A final-exposed ancestor cannot produce an independently final-qualified descendant on the same data. Formal IDs remain Coordinator allocated.

## Root-cause diagnosis and corrective action

For each actual problem, apply SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md#root-cause-diagnosis-and-repair during the active task, including first occurrences. Keep the existing Candidate/Parent/Incident Family/Partition/Exposures lineage; incident family/exposure metadata is not a recurrence-rate denominator or proof of prevention.

- Problem and Expected/Observed: <source/revision, impact, sequence, facts versus inference and missing evidence>
- Containment and Authority: <immediate effects reconciled; responsible owner; project/organization boundary>
- Causal Chain: <trigger, proximate cause, systemic cause, contributing conditions and escaped control, with evidence per link>
- Alternatives and Counterfactual: <competing hypotheses; discriminating observations; why proposed repair would prevent/detect this failure>
- Cause-to-Repair Mapping: <each supported cause/control, durable action or justified no-change finding, owner and actual grant>
- Qualification: <retained failure, fixed rubric/prior evaluator, candidate and ordinary-work counterexamples, fresh behavior where applicable, all failed/unknown observations>
- Unresolved Work: <missing authority/evidence, named owner, nonterminal blocker and exact next check; no invented root>
- Approval and Delivery: <independent Reviewer/Chief of Staff, exact candidate/evidence and verified owning-main result>
- Recurrence Follow-up: <owner and next eligible active-work trigger, availability/loading/application/effectiveness, exposure/recurrence observations or unknowns>
- Closure Decision: <supported qualified repair or independently justified no-change outcome; no guaranteed nonrecurrence claim>
