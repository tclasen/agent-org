# Implementation brief schema

- Target System: organization
- Change Classification: organization
- Authorized Scope: agent-org/shared/templates/IMPLEMENTATION_BRIEF.md
- Affected Paths or Resources: agent-org/shared/templates/IMPLEMENTATION_BRIEF.md
- Owning Role: Coordinator
- Related Requirements: REQ-000059
- Related Tasks: TASK-000243
- Approval Requirements: operator incorporation request; APP-000448; independent exact-candidate review and Chief of Staff approval before normal PR/main delivery

Copy into an authorized task/plan or its owning artifact and replace placeholders, including the eight metadata fields, with actual scope. This format grants no execution, result or approval. Inline fields may link existing canonical records. Follow [design review](../DESIGN_REVIEW.md).

- Brief Revision and Hash: <revision; content identity>
- Canonical Task and Requirements: <links and accepted revisions>
- Authority and Exclusions: <grant/approval links; writable/protected surface>
- Trigger Assessment: <material design reason or routine non-trigger reason>
- User Outcome: <what changes for whom>
- Goals and Non-Goals: <accepted behavior and explicit exclusions>
- Success Journey: <starting state; trigger; expected result; verification>
- Failure Journeys: <relevant failure/edge states, results and checks>
- Proposed Approach: <components and data/control flow>
- Reuse and Alternatives: <existing capability; alternatives; choice rationale>
- Interfaces and Affected Files: <exact scope and behavior; source links>
- Compatibility and Migration: <obligations or justified not applicable>
- Verification and Rollback: <observable checks; restoration procedure/owner>
- Open Decisions: <question; blocking/delegated/deferred; owner; decision link>
- Source Manifest: <required inputs with revisions/hashes and reading reasons>
- Review and Readiness: <fresh review link; pending until independent evidence>
- Next Action: <bounded action and its authority prerequisites>
