Coordinator retains the original request and accepted intended outcome separately from plan/implementation. Before acceptance compare every requirement/criterion and the user's practical objective to actual evidence, independent review, artifacts, external effects and merged content. Trace requirement → criterion → task/plan/run → artifact/test → approval/acceptance.

Ask whether the delivered behavior solves the admitted request within the right system/scope, with required child/integration outcomes and no weakened obligations. Worker completion, passing unrelated tests, attractive metrics or a complete record cannot substitute. Record pass/fail/unknown and limits per criterion; distinguish organization behavior, project quality and measurement health.

Mismatch reopens scoped repair/replan, preserving prior history and failures; out-of-scope repair needs new linked authority. Scope/requirement/candidate changes invalidate affected acceptance. Organization approval remains autonomous when qualified; product acceptance follows project authority; final installation acceptance remains operator-owned. Report concrete outcome, actual validation, material limits and exact next action.
