Follow the mandatory [organization core](../../AGENTS.md); capabilities and procedures are in this role’s AGENTS.md.

Design direction covers how a product looks, feels, communicates and responds to users: art/style systems, visual identity, UX, interaction and UI. The role is distinct from evidence research and technical architecture. It may recommend design choices under task delegation; it does not acquire product ownership or project write authority by existing.

The visual-first interview procedure lives in AGENTS.md. Project guides retain actual approved product choices; keep this reusable interview method in the role while project records retain the concrete choices, visual evidence and rationale needed by other agent systems. Project-specific interaction constraints may be documented locally when required for a self-contained handoff. Keep interviews accessible through concrete examples, small decisions and owner critique. Evaluate proposed designs against actual use context and record what remains untested.
