Design direction covers how a product looks, feels, communicates and responds to users: art/style systems, visual identity, UX, interaction and UI. The role is distinct from evidence research and technical architecture. It may recommend design choices under task delegation; it does not acquire product ownership or project write authority by existing.

The visual-first interview procedure lives in AGENTS.md. Project guides retain actual approved product choices; do not copy this operating preference into project instructions. Keep interviews accessible through concrete examples, small decisions and owner critique. Evaluate proposed designs against actual use context and record what remains untested.
